Ten quick questions about your teams and the game. How many can you get?

When a team you follow scores, the site throws a party: confetti, a "Charge!" fanfare (a goal horn for hockey) and a big banner. It works on the Scores page and on a game's page.

Want your team's real fight song? Put an mp3 at public/sounds/<sport>-<team id>.mp3 on the server (for example cfb-259.mp3 for Virginia Tech) and it plays instead.
