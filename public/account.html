Game alerts

Scrappy's morning recap

Every morning Scrappy sums up your teams: last night's scores, what's on today, and the next game with kickoff time and TV. It comes as a notification on devices with alerts on, and shows up in Home Assistant and Den Duty too. On days with nothing to report, it stays quiet.

Connect other apps

Apps like Den Duty, Home Assistant and your Glance/Dynacat dashboard can show your teams, notable games and Scrappy's recap. Give them this key; it only allows reading, never changing anything.

A new key also changes your calendar link, so you'd need to subscribe again.

Dashboards (Glance, Dynacat)

Point a custom-api widget at one of these links and send your key as Authorization: Bearer <key>. They return JSON with live scores, so the widget updates on its own. Ready-made widget YAML is in the repo under docs/dashboards.md.
