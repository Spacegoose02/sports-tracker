Stat Scraps now opens on a home page for every sport, and notable games move into your own account, with a private key for dashboard widgets.

## Upgrade

```
docker pull ghcr.io/spacegoose02/sports-tracker:1.3.0
```

On Unraid, click **Update** on the container. Your data in appdata carries over. There are no new settings in this release.

Heads up:
- Scores have moved from `/` to `/scores`. Old links to `index.html` still work.
- The admin's notable picks are still the default for signed-out visitors, and `/api/notable/feed` still serves them, so existing Dynacat/Glance widgets keep working. To show your own picks instead, switch the widget to the new per-account feed (see below).

## What's new

**An all-sports home page**
- `/` is now a front page for every sport: your followed teams from all leagues, the closest live games, a card per in-season league with its top games, and mixed headlines (#39)
- Each sport's scoreboard lives at `/scores`. The nav gets a Home link, the logo goes home, and switching sport from a team or game page goes to that sport's scores (#39)

**Your own notable games**
- Star any game card to add it to your notable games. The Notable tab shows your list (#38)
- The admin's picks become the site default for signed-out visitors, and you can copy them into your account (#38)

**Dashboards**
- Read-only endpoints for Glance/Dynacat widgets, authenticated with the private key on your Account page: `/api/account/notable/feed`, `/api/account/dashboard` and `/api/account/notable` (#38)
- A new My Teams widget, and `dynacat-notable-games-widget.yaml` now reads your own picks. Setup is in `docs/dashboards.md` (#38)
