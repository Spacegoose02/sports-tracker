Stat Scraps adds soccer, Formula 1 and golf, game-day games for kids, multiview, award races and opt-in game alerts, and fixes live scores on My Teams cards.

## Upgrade

```
docker pull ghcr.io/spacegoose02/sports-tracker:1.4.0
```

On Unraid, click **Update** on the container. Your data in appdata carries over. There are no new settings in this release. Advanced college football stats use the `CFBD_API_KEY` you may already have set for recruiting.

## What's new

**More sports**
- Premier League, MLS and Champions League, with league tables sorted by points and goals for/against, plus soccer explanations, trivia and a goal horn for kids (#42)
- Formula 1 race weekends and PGA Tour leaderboards with scores to par (#42)

**Watching games**
- Multiview: two or four games at once, filled with the best live games or your picks, with a shareable link (#42)
- Award races: Heisman watch and the NFL MVP race, projected from season stat leaders with weekly movement arrows (#42)
- Advanced college football stats: SP+, EPA per play, success rate, explosiveness and havoc rate on team pages, and an advanced box score on game pages (#42)
- Opt-in alerts for every score by a team you follow, close games late, and upsets brewing. All are off until you turn them on (#42)

**Kids and family**
- A Play tab on every game page: Game Day Bingo that fills itself from the play-by-play, "Call it!" next-play guesses, final score guesses and collectible trading cards (#42)
- XP, levels, daily streaks and unlockable outfits for Scrappy (#42)
- Watch party chat for your family league on each game page (#42)
- In kid mode, Scrappy reads your scores and "What just happened?" out loud, using the browser's own voice (#42)

## Fixes

- My Teams cards now show the live score and clock for games in progress in every sport, instead of a dash. The same fix covers the Scores page and team pages (#41)
