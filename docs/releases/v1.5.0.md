News and videos now follow whichever sport you pick, and college football news pulls from more sources.

## Upgrade

```
docker pull ghcr.io/spacegoose02/sports-tracker:1.5.0
```

On Unraid, click **Update** on the container. Your data in appdata carries over. There are no new settings in this release.

## What's new

**News**
- College football news adds On3, 247Sports, The Athletic and SI alongside ESPN, and other sports add more stories to ESPN's league headlines. Duplicate stories are merged (#44)
- A team and conference picker on the News tab, built from the standings (#44)
- Stories without an image show as compact rows instead of a big empty frame (#44)

**Videos**
- The Videos tab follows the selected sport, with official league channels for the NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, Premier League and F1. Sports without a channel hide the tab (#44)
- Game highlights come from the sport's own channels (#44)
