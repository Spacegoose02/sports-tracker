Link Tinyauth and other single sign-on logins to the accounts people already have, from the account page or from Admin.

## Upgrade

```
docker pull ghcr.io/spacegoose02/sports-tracker:1.8.0
```

On Unraid, click **Update** on the container. Your data in appdata carries over. There are no new settings.

On first start, 1.8.0 moves existing single sign-on links into the new sign-in links table, so everyone who signs in with Tinyauth today stays linked to the same account.

## What's new

**Your account**
- A new **Ways to sign in** section on the account page lists your password and every linked login (#50)
- **Link <provider>** buttons link a login to your account even when the emails don't match. If that login was on another account, it moves over to yours (#50)
- **Unlink** removes a login, but never your last way to sign in (#50)

**Admin**
- **Admin > Users** lists every account with its logins (#50)
- Make a one-time link, good for 7 days, that attaches a provider login to a given account. You can also move a login to another account or unlink it (#50)
