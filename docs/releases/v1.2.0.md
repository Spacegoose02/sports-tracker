Stat Scraps gets a new front page. The home page, navigation and score cards are rebuilt, and this release adds a conference race tracker, a CFP "play it out" picker, against-the-spread pick'em, site-wide search and richer cards for other sports.

## Upgrade

```
docker pull ghcr.io/spacegoose02/sports-tracker:1.2.0
```

On Unraid, click **Update** on the container. Your data in appdata carries over. There are no new settings in this release.

## What's new

**A new front page**
- One nav on every page (Scores, Rankings, Standings, Playoffs and a More menu), with a bottom tab bar on phones (#33)
- Home page with a My Teams hero, a week stepper, games grouped into Your teams / Live now / Coming up / Final, Top 25 and Live filters, and top headlines (#33)
- Bolder score cards with team color stripes, down and distance on live games, and "Games to watch" tags like Upset alert and Nail-biter (#33)
- A live ticker of followed and ranked games along the bottom of inner pages (#33)
- Site-wide search for teams, players and pages, from the header or with `/` and Ctrl+K (#36)

**College football**
- Conference race tracker: each team's title-game status, from Clinched to Out, linked from the More menu (#34)
- Game page split into Gamecast, Box Score, Matchup and Watch & Listen tabs (#33)
- "Play it out" on the projected CFP field: tap winners through every round to crown a champion (#33)

**Pick'em**
- Against-the-spread mode next to straight up, with pushes on an exact line. Hidden in kid mode (#35)

**Other sports**
- Baseball cards draw the bases and the count; MLB, NHL, NBA, WNBA and college basketball cards get line scores (#35)

**Admin**
- A Watch on BingeBandit setup check on the admin page that names the first missing step (#32)
